##Siapkan Apache Benchmark di Kusakabe

Jalankan di terminal Kusakabe:
apk update
apk add apache2-utils curl bind-tools


nano /etc/nginx/sites-available/soal16-public-gateway
kasih comment(#) di limit_req untuk pengujian baseline terlebih dahulu, kalo yg pas pengecekan setelah dipasang rate limitingnya, tinggal 
diilangin lagi aja commentnya 
-baseline
./test20
cp -r /root/soal20-results /root/soal20-baseline
grep -E \
'Complete requests:|Failed requests:|Requests per second:|Time per request:|Non-2xx responses:' \
/root/soal20-baseline/*.txt
-rate limiting
./test20
cp -r /root/soal20-results /root/soal20-rate-limit
grep -E \
'Complete requests:|Failed requests:|Requests per second:|Time per request:|Non-2xx responses:' \
/root/soal20-rate-limit/*.txt
